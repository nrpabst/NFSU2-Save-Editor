NFSU2 Save Editor

Edit cash, garage cars and parts in a Need for Speed Underground 2 profile. Everything runs in this page; your file never leaves your computer.

1. Open your profile

Drop your profile file here (the one named after your profile, e.g. neuneu inside NFS Underground 2/neuneu/) or click to choose it.

Keep a copy of the original. This tool doesn't touch GARAGE.DAT or the other backup files.

Bank

Values above about 10 million may not display properly in the game's menus.

Career progress

This copies the whole career state from the 100% save: races won, stages, unlocked shops, parts and cars, sponsors, stats and records. Your cars, cash, current car, profile name and game settings stay as they are. You can't pick a single stage yet, because I haven't worked out which bytes mean what. Your own race history is replaced, so keep your backup.

Career garage

My Cars (Quick Race garage)

20 slots for cars you can use in Quick Race. Copy a career car here, or start a new stock car. This part is less tested than the career garage.

or

Part hash calculator

NFSU2 stores every part as a hash of its name. Type a part name to see its hash; you can paste a name straight into the part table too.
