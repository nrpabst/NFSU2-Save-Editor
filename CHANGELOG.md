# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Dyno tuning: every slider from the game's tuning screen, for each of the 5 race-type setups (Circuit and Sprint, Drag, Drift, Street X, URL). That covers suspension, ride height, steering, gears, aero, grip, brake bias, nitrous, and the ECU and turbo torque curves.
- Tuning presets: Grip, Drag, Drift and Top speed. Apply one to a setup, or apply each to its own race type in one click.

## 1.0.1

- Fixed the car picker spilling out of empty career garage slots.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Set your cash.
- Unlock 100% career progress and keep your own cars.
- Add, swap or remove career garage cars, stock or as one of 12 fully modded builds.
- Max out performance, and change body kits, widebodies, hoods, lights and more.
- Edit the Quick Race garage (My Cars).
- Writes valid checksums, so nfsu2.xyz doesn't flag the save as edited.
- Windows app that updates itself, plus the same editor as an HTML file and online.
